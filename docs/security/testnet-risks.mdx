description: "Limits of Arc Testnet deployments, assets, and availability."
---

Cooket currently operates in Arc Testnet development and testing.

<Warning>
  Arc Testnet tokens and USDC do not represent real financial value. Do not purchase testnet balances from strangers or treat testnet market values as realizable assets.
</Warning>

Testnets can be reset, upgraded, paused, reorganized, congested, or unavailable. Faucets, RPC services, explorers, contracts, and third-party infrastructure may change without production-grade continuity guarantees. Transaction inclusion and indexing can be delayed.

Arc Testnet deployment does not imply Arc Mainnet deployment or compatibility. The addresses in this documentation are testnet-only. Never reuse them on another chain without independently verifying chain ID and bytecode.

The repository's Compose and Vercel configuration shows supported deployment shapes; it does not prove current public health, uptime, capacity, latency, or data completeness.
