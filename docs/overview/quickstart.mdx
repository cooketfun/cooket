---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Connect an external wallet and explore Cooket safely."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the canonical application">
    Visit [cooket.fun](https://cooket.fun). Verify the exact origin before connecting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an external wallet">
    Use the Reown AppKit wallet connection. Cooket does not create or custody a wallet for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Arc Testnet">
    Confirm chain ID `5042002`, RPC `https://rpc.testnet.arc.io`, and explorer `https://testnet.arcscan.app`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore or transact">
    Browse indexed launches without signing. If you create or trade, review the target, asset representation, value, allowance, slippage, and deadline in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the result">
    Preserve the transaction hash and inspect it on ArcScan. Indexed application data can lag the receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Arc native USDC is the 18-decimal value and gas asset used by bonding-curve trades. Canonical ERC-20 USDC is a 6-decimal token used by graduated Uniswap V3 markets.

Continue with [Connect wallet](/docs/using-cooket/connect-wallet), [Create a token](/docs/using-cooket/create-token), or [Trading terminal](/docs/using-cooket/trading-terminal).
